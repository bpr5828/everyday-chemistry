Clinical Trials Mentorship Program Planner

Audience: High-school students with no prior knowledge of clinical trials or clinical data.

Program Goal: Introduce students to how medicines are tested, how study data is collected and standardized, and how simple Python checks can be used to find data quality issues.

Format: 2 weeks, beginner-friendly, using public learning materials and public sample datasets.

Week 1 • Foundations

Week 1 Objective

Build basic understanding of clinical trials, patient data collection, data standards, and why data quality matters.

Expected Week Outcome
Students can explain the clinical trial lifecycle, describe what study data looks like, and recognize why organized, standardized data is important.
Week 2 • Application

Week 2 Objective

Move from concepts into practice by reading simple datasets, defining data checks, and building a beginner-level Python validator.

Expected Week Outcome
Students can load a CSV file, understand basic validation rules, and demonstrate a small Python project that detects data issues.
Week 1

Day 1: What Is a Clinical Trial?

Tasks
  • Learn what clinical trials are and why they are conducted.
  • Understand the basic trial phases I-IV at a beginner level.
  • Create a simple journey map: idea → testing → approval → patient use.
Expected Outcome

Student can explain, in simple language, how a medicine is tested before public use.

Day 2: How Clinical Data Is Collected

Tasks
  • Learn what patient study data looks like.
  • Review a mock Case Report Form (CRF).
  • Identify common fields such as patient ID, visit, age, sex, and measurements.

Day 3: Why Data Standards Matter

Tasks
  • Compare messy data with standardized data.
  • Discuss why consistency matters in science and healthcare.
  • Create a small raw-data versus clean-data comparison chart.
Expected Outcome

Day 4: SDTM Basics

Tasks

Day 10: Final Presentation and Demo

Final Project

Expected Final Project Output

  • Python Script: Reads at least 2 sample datasets such as DM and VS or AE.
  • Validation Rules Implemented: 5 simple checks including unique ID, required fields, date format, dataset/domain match, and a basic status rule.
  • Output Report: Printed summary showing PASS/FAIL and number of issues found.
  • Presentation Deck: 5-8 slides explaining clinical trials basics, dataset used, validation rules, code approach, and demo results.
  • Demo Evidence: Screenshot or notebook output showing errors detected in the sample data.
Sample Final Output Format
Validation Summary
------------------
USUBJID uniqueness: FAILED (2 duplicates)
Required fields: PASSED
Date format: FAILED (3 invalid dates)
Domain check: PASSED
Status rule: FAILED (2 invalid entries)
